## About Me

Welcome! I'm **Rafid Ishrak Jahan**, a second-year Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the **University of North Texas**, advised by **Dr. Sagnik Ray Choudhury**.

My research focuses on **Large Language Model (LLM) evaluation**, **Natural Language Processing (NLP)**, **Human Preference Learning**, and **Explainable AI**. I am particularly interested in developing reliable evaluation methodologies, preference datasets, and interpretable systems for modern language models.

Before joining UNT, I worked as a Software Engineer at Infosys, where I developed backend systems using Java and Spring Boot.
